THE REDUCTION LADDER · WALL CHART

A hundred and thirty-four inter-theory relations

An arrow means the upper theory stands in the named relation to the lower one. The kinds are not interchangeable: a limit is a regular expansion in a small dimensionless parameter; a singular limit does not exist pointwise, and the interesting physics lives where it fails; an effective theory arrow integrates out heavy degrees of freedom with a computable error; coarse-grain averages over degrees of freedom; constitutive is composition rather than derivation; contested marks a standard reduction claim the literature does not support; formal analogy marks shared mathematics with no reduction relation at all. A few arrows run upward, from the coarser level to the finer one.
